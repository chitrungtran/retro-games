Tarot card sound effects
=======================

Source pack: Casino Audio (1.1) by Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl)
  https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio
  Also listed on OpenGameArt.org as "54 Casino sound effects (cards, dice, chips)"

License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

You may use these assets in personal and commercial projects.
Credit (Kenney or www.kenney.nl) would be nice but is not mandatory.

The files in this folder were trimmed, faded, and re-encoded
(mono, 22.05 kHz, Ogg Vorbis + MP3) from the original pack:

  shuffle  <- Audio/card-shuffle.ogg (trimmed to about 1.85s)
  draw     <- Audio/card-slide-1.ogg
  place    <- Audio/card-place-2.ogg
  flip     <- Audio/card-fan-1.ogg (crisp ending)
  shimmer  <- Audio/card-fan-2.ogg (pitched, filtered, short echo)
